The Story of Elmak Gardens

A Hidden Gem in Nature's Embrace

Nestled along the gentle banks of a crystalline river, Elmak Gardens is more than a venue — it's a living canvas where manicured lawns meet wildflower meadows, and every breeze carries the scent of blooming jasmine and fresh earth.

Our gardens were designed with intention: to create a space where celebrations feel effortless, where the boundary between indoors and outdoors dissolves, and where every guest feels the warmth of nature's hospitality.

The River That Runs Through

A gentle stream of serenity

Connected to Nature

A small, crystal-clear river winds along the edge of Elmak Gardens, its soft babbling providing the perfect natural soundtrack to your event. The riverbank is lined with weeping willows and wild irises, creating secluded nooks perfect for quiet reflection or stunning photographs.

Guests can wander down to the water's edge, dip their toes in the cool stream on warm afternoons, or simply sit and watch the dragonflies dance across the surface.

Amenities & Experiences

Garden-to-Table Dining

Fresh, seasonal cuisine prepared with herbs and vegetables from our own garden, served al fresco under the open sky.

Pool & Games Lounge

A stylish recreational space featuring a pool table, board games, and a cozy lounge — perfect for guests to unwind between events.

Relaxation Nooks

Scattered throughout the gardens are hammocks, daybeds, and shaded seating areas designed for quiet moments of peace.

Flower Arrangement Studio

Create your own bouquets from our cutting garden, guided by our in-house florist — a delightful activity for wedding parties.

Evening Fire Pit

As night falls, gather around the stone fire pit for stories, toasted marshmallows, and starlit conversations.

Picture-Perfect Spots

Curated photo points throughout the property — from the rose archway to the river bend — ensure unforgettable memories.
